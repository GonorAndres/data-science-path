# Target Companies: Mexican DS Shortlist 2026

A curated list of Mexican employers actively hiring data scientists and ML engineers. Grouped by sector. For each: typical DS role names, rough stack signals from public job ads, where openings post, and a difficulty band for a junior-to-mid applicant.

Difficulty bands:

- **A** = Highly competitive. Needs 2+ years of solid experience or top-tier referral.
- **B** = Standard DS hiring process, achievable with strong portfolio.
- **C** = Accessible to juniors with a tight portfolio; large class of openings.

All stacks are inferred from public job postings observed in 2025 and 2026. Verify current ads before applying.

## How to use this list

1. Mark 10 companies from bands B and C that match your interest.
2. For each, bookmark the direct careers URL (not the aggregator).
3. Set up a LinkedIn saved search that includes all 10 company names.
4. Write a one-line "why this company" for each. Use it to tailor the cover letter or InMail.
5. Set a 30-day target: 3 applications per week across these 10 companies, plus opportunistic applications elsewhere.

## Notes on Mexican DS market dynamics (2026)

- Fintech and big retail dominate DS hiring volume in Mexico.
- Banks have larger analytics orgs but slower pipelines and some legacy SAS work.
- Consulting firms absorb more juniors than product firms, but the DS content varies wildly by client.
- Remote-first product companies (Belvo, Bitso, some Nu roles) widen the LATAM talent pool and raise the bar.
- English proficiency is a real filter at multinationals and product firms. B2+ opens doors that are closed otherwise.
- Referrals matter more in Mexico than US public data suggests. A warm intro inside a B-band company beats ten cold applications.
