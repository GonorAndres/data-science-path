# LinkedIn: Headline and About (Bilingual Copy)

LinkedIn is the single highest-leverage channel for Mexican data science job hunting in 2026. Recruiters at Citi, Nu, Bitso, Kavak, Coppel, and every consulting firm source candidates here first. The headline and About are what they screen on before opening the profile.

## Rules

- Headline: 220 characters max, use all of it. Lead with a role, then a differentiator, then a stack.
- About: 2,600 characters max, first 230 show above the fold. Front-load keywords.
- Feature section: pin 3 portfolio items (GitHub repo + Medium article + demo URL).
- Creator mode on if you plan to post. Off otherwise. Empty creator mode signals "I turned it on then gave up".

## Featured section

Pin three items. Recommended mix:

1. **GitHub repo**: link to this repository's root. The matrix at the top of the README is the hook.
2. **Flagship project**: direct link to `projects/nlp-reviews-multilingual/` or `projects/mlops-model-service/`.
3. **Writing sample**: a Medium or Dev.to post about one project. If you do not blog yet, pin `docs/theory/09_llm_foundations_2026.md` as a "technical essay".

## Experience section notes

- Even for internship or junior role, copy the exact achievement bullets from your CV. Rewriting them differently is wasted effort.
- List "Actuarial Science, UNAM" as education, not as experience.
- Projects can go under a "Projects" section, not Experience.

## Activity cadence (if you post)

- 2 posts per week, maximum. More than that and quality drops.
- Topics that convert: a project update ("shipped this this week"), a short technical insight (something you debugged), a recruiter-visible reflection (career decision, book takeaway).
- Do not post motivational content or repost influencer takes. Recruiters filter these out.

## ATS and search optimization

Mexican recruiters search LinkedIn for exactly these terms: "Data Scientist", "Data Science", "Machine Learning", "Cientifico de Datos", "ML Engineer", "Analista de Datos", "Actuario". Your headline and About must contain at least five of these literally. Both language variants matter.

## Audit checklist

- [ ] Photo is professional headshot, no selfie, no distracting background.

- [ ] Headline fills 200+ of the 220 characters.

- [ ] Contact info section has working URL and email.
- [ ] Open to work set to "recruiters only" (not public badge, which signals desperation).
- [ ] Skills ordered by priority.
