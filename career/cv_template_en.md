## Common mistakes

1. Listing courses without matching projects. A course without a built artifact is noise.
2. "Knowledge of Python" with no evidence. Replace with "Python, 3 years, 5 GitHub projects".
3. Objective ("I want to grow as...") instead of Summary ("I deliver X using Y with Z impact"). Recruiters care about what you deliver.
4. Experience in ascending chronological order. It goes descending: most recent at the top.
5. Icons, emojis, colored boxes that break the ATS parser. OCC converts your PDF to plain text. Anything not plain-readable is discarded.
6. Two-page CV for a junior candidate. One page until 3+ years of experience.
