# Career Toolkit (Mexican Data Science Job Market)

## Resumen (ES)

Este kit es para encontrar empleo en ciencia de datos en México en 2026. Incluye plantillas de CV en español e inglés optimizadas para ATS y OCC, copy de LinkedIn bilingüe, guía de uso de OCC Mundial / Bumeran / Indeed MX / LinkedIn MX, un shortlist de empresas que contratan DS en México, plantillas de mensajes para recruiters, y una carpeta de preparación para entrevistas (SQL, pandas, teoría ML, diseño de sistemas ML, preguntas conductuales).

Job-search toolkit for data science roles in Mexico in 2026. ## How to use this toolkit

1. **Baseline your CV.** Copy `cv_template_es.md` and `cv_template_en.md`, replace placeholders, run the ATS keyword check at the bottom of each template.
2. **Rewrite LinkedIn.** Pick one of the three headline variants. Paste the About copy with your specifics.
3. **Pick your target list.** Read `target_companies_mexico.md`, mark the 10 companies that match your level and interest, note the direct careers URL for each.
4. **Set up job-board searches.** Follow `mexican_ds_job_boards.md` to configure daily alerts on OCC, LinkedIn MX, Bumeran, Indeed MX.
5. **Practice interviews.** Work through `interview_prep/` in this order: SQL drills, pandas drills, ML flashcards, then one case study per day for a week.
6. **Outreach.** Use `recruiter_outreach_templates.md` for three cold DMs per day during active search.

## Calibration notes for 2026

- Junior DS in CDMX posts at MXN $8,000 to $14,000 per month; mid-level at $18,000 to $32,000; senior at $35,000 to $60,000+. Bilingual role premium is real (5 to 15 percent).
- English level: most multinationals want B2 to C1. Verified via interview, not just CV.
- Remote and hybrid roles now common but in-office CDMX roles still dominate for junior.
- The portfolio is screened first. A LinkedIn with no pinned repo and a CV with only coursework is filtered out early. Keep the GitHub landing page tight and pin three projects.
