# Recruiter Outreach Templates (ES + EN)

Cold outreach works in Mexican DS job hunting when it is short, personalized, and specific. These templates are battle-tested patterns; swap placeholders for real names, role details, and one concrete reason for contacting that person.

## Principles

1. Never send templates verbatim. Personalize the first line so it cannot be sent to any other person.
2. Name one specific thing (product, team, post, project) you researched about the recipient. No generic flattery.
3. Ask for one small action (15-minute call, quick reply to a question, referral intro).
4. Make the subject or first line clearly state what you want.
5. Keep under 120 words. Long DMs get skipped.

## When to use DM vs email vs InMail

| Channel | When to use |
|---------|-------------|
| LinkedIn connection note (300 chars) | First touch if profile is reachable. |
| LinkedIn DM (no limit) | Once connection is accepted, or to recruiters who follow you. |
| LinkedIn InMail (paid) | Only if target does not accept your connection within 5 days and role is high priority. |
| Email | When you have the address via company careers page or public directory. |
| Twitter / X DM | Rarely. Works for startup founders and niche tech community. |

## Templates

### 1. Cold to recruiter, in Spanish (connection note, 300 chars)

```
Hola [Nombre], soy actuario UNAM enfocado en data science. Vi que reclutas para [empresa] y me interesa mucho el rol de [titulo exacto]. Mi portafolio: github.com/GonorAndres/data-science-path. Agradeceria mucho si podemos conectar, y si aplica, una llamada corta esta semana. Gracias.
```

Character count check: 298 including spaces. If the company name is long, trim "mucho" and "corta esta semana" to fit.

### 2. Cold to recruiter, in English (connection note)

```
Hi [Name], UNAM actuary focused on data science here. Saw you recruit for [company] and I'm interested in the [exact role title] opening. My portfolio: github.com/GonorAndres/data-science-path. Would love to connect, and if there's fit, a short call this week. Thanks.
```

### 3. Cold to hiring manager / DS team lead (DM, 120 words)

**ES:**

```
Hola [Nombre],

Vi tu post sobre [tema especifico, ej. el experimento de forecasting que publicaste la semana pasada] y me llamo la atencion [detalle concreto]. Soy actuario UNAM y tengo un portafolio de proyectos de data science puros, incluyendo uno de forecasting jerarquico sobre M5 que llego a WAPE 9.2 en top-down. Enlace: github.com/GonorAndres/data-science-path

No busco contratacion inmediata, solo me interesa mucho el tipo de problemas que resuelven en [equipo]. Si tienes 15 minutos en las proximas dos semanas para una llamada breve, te lo agradeceria mucho.

Saludos,
Andres
```

**EN:**

```
Hi [Name],

Saw your post about [specific topic, e.g. the forecasting experiment you shared last week] and the point about [concrete detail] caught my attention. I'm a UNAM actuary with a data science portfolio, including a hierarchical forecasting project on M5 that hit 9.2 WAPE top-down. Link: github.com/GonorAndres/data-science-path

Not asking about hiring right now, just very interested in the kinds of problems your team is solving at [team]. If you have 15 minutes in the next couple weeks for a quick call, I'd really appreciate it.

Thanks,
Andres
```

Hope you are well. I'm applying for the [exact title] role at [company]. I saw you have been there for [time] and was wondering if you'd have 10 minutes to share your experience on the [area] team, and if it seems like a fit, introduce me to [specific person if you know one].

**ES:**

```
Hola [Nombre],

Gracias por el tiempo de hoy. Me quede pensando en la pregunta sobre [tema especifico de la entrevista], y queria agregar un punto que no desarrolle bien en vivo: [1 a 2 lineas con la respuesta mejorada].

Saludos,

```

### 8. Polite decline and relationship preservation (when declining an offer)

**ES:**

```
Hola [Nombre],

Muchas gracias por la oferta y por todo el proceso. Despues de pensarlo con cuidado, decidi aceptar una oportunidad que alinea mas con [razon generica, ej. "el stack que busco" o "la estructura del equipo"]. Valoro mucho el tiempo que invirtieron conmigo y espero mantener el contacto.

Saludos cordiales,
Andres
```

**EN:**

```
Hi [Name],

Thank you for the offer and for the full process. After careful thought, I decided to accept an opportunity that aligns more with [generic reason, e.g. "the stack I am targeting" or "the team structure"]. I really appreciate the time your team invested with me and hope we can stay in touch.

Best regards,
Andres
```

## Customization checklist before sending

- [ ] First line mentions something specific to the recipient (post, project, team, role).
- [ ] Portfolio and CV URLs are live.
- [ ] Recipient name spelled correctly. No "Hola [Nombre]" left in production.
- [ ] Company name accurate (Nu, not Nubank MX. Citi, not Banamex for international roles).
- [ ] Action is clear (15-min call, referral intro, confirmation, next-step request).
- [ ] Under 120 words for DM; under 300 chars for connection note.
- [ ] Language matches the recipient's preferred language (check their profile posts).

## What not to do

1. Do not attach your CV in a cold DM. Link to LinkedIn or portfolio, let them pull if interested.
2. Do not copy-paste the same DM to 20 people on the same day. LinkedIn detects patterns and throttles.
3. Do not send at weird hours (3am, Sundays). Best windows: Tuesday to Thursday, 09:00 to 11:00 CDMX time.
4. Do not follow up more than twice. If two messages get silence, move on.
5. Do not name-drop unless genuine. "Hi, I'm friends with X" when X barely knows you is easily verified and burns both relationships.

## Response tracking

Log every outreach in the same spreadsheet as job applications:

| Date | Channel | Recipient | Company | Type (cold, followup, etc) | Outcome | Next step |

Goal: 20 outreach messages per week during active search, 5 per week during passive search.
